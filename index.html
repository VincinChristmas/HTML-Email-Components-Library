What we learned from 400 packing lists

We asked customers to photograph everything they packed for a week away, then asked afterwards what they never touched. The overlap was bigger than anyone expected.

Nine items showed up on almost every list and came home unused. Cutting those nine is the difference between a bag you carry and a bag you drag.
